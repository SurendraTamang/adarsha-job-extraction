# Growth Plan: 4k → 50k Followers

## Current State
- **Followers**: 4,000
- **Platform**: Facebook Page

## Growth Phases

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1-2)
**Goal**: 4k → 6k followers

### Focus
- Consistent posting schedule
- Build trust with quality jobs
- Understand what content works

### Actions
| Week | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Set up Google Form for employers |
| 1 | Create Canva templates |
| 1 | Post first 10 jobs |
| 2 | Reach out to 10 local employers |
| 2 | Join 5 Pokhara Facebook groups |
| 3 | Start weekly job roundups |
| 3 | First engagement poll |
| 4 | Review analytics, adjust strategy |

### Tactics
1. **Employer Outreach**
   - Visit 5 hotels personally
   - Contact 5 IT companies via email/call
   - Ask friends who own businesses

2. **Group Sharing**
   - Share jobs in Pokhara groups (without spamming)
   - Be helpful in groups, build reputation
   - Follow group rules strictly

3. **Consistency**
   - Post at same times daily
   - Never miss a day
   - Quality over quantity

### Metrics Target
- 50 jobs posted
- 500 new followers
- 10 employers contacted

## Growth Hacks & Quick Wins

### Free Tactics
1. **Contests**
   - "Best CV wins Rs. 1000"
   - Tag & share to enter
   - Low cost, high engagement

2. **User-Generated Content**
   - "Share your first job story"
   - Feature follower stories
   - Screenshot testimonials

3. **Timing Hacks**
   - Post when competitors don't
   - Job postings on Monday morning
   - Weekend engagement posts

4. **Psychological Triggers**
   - Urgency ("Only 2 days left to apply!")
   - Social proof ("500 people applied")
   - FOMO ("This job won't last long")

### Paid Tactics (When Budget Allows)
1. **Boosted Posts**: Rs. 500-1000 per post
2. **Page Like Campaigns**: Target local audience
3. **Retargeting**: Website visitors → Facebook
4. **Influencer Collabs**: Local Pokhara influencers

## Competitive Moat Building

### How to Stay Ahead of Copycats

1. **Speed**: Be first to post new jobs
2. **Trust**: Verified employers only
3. **Community**: People follow YOU, not just jobs
4. **Relationships**: Exclusive employer partnerships
5. **Brand**: "Job Solution Pokhara" = jobs in Pokhara

---

## Risk Management

### If Growth Stalls
- Survey followers for feedback
- Try new content format
- Increase ad spend temporarily
- Partner with bigger page

### If Competitor Emerges
- Focus on quality, not just quantity
- Highlight your track record
- Lock in employer partnerships
- Consider strategic partnership/merger

### If Facebook Reach Drops
- Diversify to Instagram/TikTok
- Push website and SEO harder
- Build email list aggressively
- WhatsApp community

## Monthly Review Template

### Questions to Ask Each Month
1. How many jobs did we post?
2. What content performed best?
3. How many new followers gained?
4. How many employer leads?
5. What revenue generated?
6. What should we do differently?

### Adjust Strategy Based On
- Best performing post types → do more
- Worst performing → stop or improve
- Employer feedback → what they want
- Follower comments → what they need
