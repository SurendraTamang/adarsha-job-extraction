# Content Strategy

## Content Pillars

### 1. Job Listings (60% of content)
The core - why people follow you

### 2. Job Seeker Tips (20% of content)
Value-add content that builds trust

### 3. Engagement Posts (15% of content)
Polls, questions, discussions

### 4. Success Stories (5% of content)
Social proof when someone gets hired

## Engagement Tactics

### Reply Strategy
- Reply to EVERY comment within 2 hours
- Ask follow-up questions
- Tag relevant people when appropriate
- Thank people for sharing

### Community Building
- Ask followers to share posts
- Create sense of community ("हाम्रो job family")
- Celebrate milestones (1k likes, 100th job posted)
- Remember repeat commenters by name

### Call-to-Actions to Use
- "Share with someone who needs this!"
- "Tag a friend looking for work"
- "Save this post for later"
- "Comment if you applied!"
- "What salary would you expect for this?"

## Content Don'ts

❌ Don't post:
- Jobs without verifying employer
- Negative content about employers
- Political content
- Controversial opinions
- Too many posts in one day (spam feeling)
- Posts without images (low reach)

2. Create graphics in Canva (batch)

4. Schedule using Facebook Business Suite

### Monthly Prep
1. Create 4 tip posts
2. Create 4 engagement posts
3. Plan any special campaigns
4. Review what worked last month

---

## Language Guidelines

### Mix Nepali + English Naturally
- Headlines can be English (professional feel)
- Details in Nepali or mixed
- Match how Pokhara people actually talk

### Tone
- Friendly but professional
- Helpful, not salesy
- Local, relatable
- Encouraging for job seekers

### Examples
✅ "यो opportunity miss नगर्नुहोस्!"
✅ "Apply गर्न interested?"
❌ "BEST JOB EVER!!!" (too salesy)
❌ Pure formal Nepali (not how people talk)
