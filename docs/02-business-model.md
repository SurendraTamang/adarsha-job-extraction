# Business Model

## Vision
Become the go-to platform for jobs in Pokhara - where employers post first and job seekers check daily.

## Value Proposition

### For Job Seekers (Free)
- Local jobs in one place (no filtering through Kathmandu-centric sites)
- Verified, legitimate listings
- Easy to access via Facebook (no app download needed)

### For Employers
- Access to local talent pool
- Affordable compared to traditional advertising
- Quick turnaround - post today, get applicants tomorrow

---

## Revenue Model

### Phase 1: Free (Months 1-3)
**Goal**: Build volume, trust, and habit

- All job postings FREE
- Build reputation as reliable source
- Collect data on what employers want
- Target: 100+ jobs posted, 10k+ followers

### Phase 2: Freemium (Months 4-6)
**Goal**: Start generating revenue

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Basic | Free | Standard listing, 7-day visibility |
| Featured | Rs. 500/post | Top placement, highlighted, 14 days |
| Urgent | Rs. 300/post | "Urgent Hiring" badge |

### Phase 3: Subscriptions (Month 6+)
**Goal**: Recurring revenue from regular employers

| Plan | Price/Month | Features |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Starter | Rs. 1,000 | 5 job posts |
| Business | Rs. 2,500 | 15 job posts + featured placement |
| Enterprise | Rs. 5,000 | Unlimited posts + priority support |

## Competitive Advantage

### Why Choose Job Solution Pokhara?

1. **Local Focus**
   - MeroJob = Kathmandu-centric
   - You = Pokhara-only = more relevant

2. **Facebook Native**
   - No app download required
   - Where your audience already is
   - Easy sharing in local groups

3. **Personal Touch**
   - You know Pokhara
   - Employers can WhatsApp you directly
   - Trust factor vs. anonymous platforms

4. **Speed**
   - Post same day
   - No lengthy verification process
   - Urgent hirings handled quickly

## Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Low employer adoption | No jobs to post | Personally reach out to 20 employers |
| Fake/scam jobs | Reputation damage | Strict verification SOP |
| Competitor copies idea | Market share loss | Build brand loyalty first |
| Facebook algorithm changes | Reach drops | Build email list, website |
| Your time constraints | Inconsistent posting | Batch content, schedule posts |

## Exit Strategies (Long-term)

1. **Keep running** - Lifestyle business, passive income
2. **Expand** - Other cities (Butwal, Bharatpur, Chitwan)
3. **Sell** - To larger job platform wanting local presence
4. **Merge** - Partner with complementary service

## Decision Points

### When to Invest More Time
- Consistent 50+ jobs/month
- Employers asking to pay
- 10k+ engaged followers

### When to Build Website
- Facebook limitations become bottleneck
- Need searchable job database
- Ready for SEO traffic

### When to Hire Help
- Revenue covers Rs. 10,000+/month salary
- You can't handle volume alone
- Quality suffering due to time
