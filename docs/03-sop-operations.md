# Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## Daily Operations

### Morning Routine (30 mins)
1. Check employer submissions (Google Form/Email)
2. Verify job listings are legitimate
3. Format and post 2-3 jobs to Facebook
4. Respond to any messages/comments

### Evening Routine (15 mins)
1. Check engagement on posts
2. Reply to comments/inquiries
3. Note what type of posts performed well

---

## SOP 1: Receiving Job Listings from Employers

### Via Google Form
1. Employer fills out form with:
   - Company name
   - Job title
   - Job description
   - Requirements
   - Salary range (optional)
   - Application deadline
   - Contact method (email/phone/visit)

2. You receive email notification
3. Review for completeness and legitimacy
4. Add to posting queue

### Via Facebook Message
1. Ask employer to provide all required details
2. Or send them the Google Form link
3. Confirm details before posting

### Verification Checklist
- [ ] Company name sounds legitimate
- [ ] Contact information provided
- [ ] Job description is clear
- [ ] No suspicious requests (no fees, no personal data)
- [ ] Salary seems reasonable for role

## SOP 2: Creating Facebook Job Posts

### Post Format Template

```
🔵 [JOB TITLE] - [COMPANY NAME]
📍 Location: [Area, Pokhara]

[Brief job description - 2-3 lines]

✅ Requirements:
• [Requirement 1]
• [Requirement 2]
• [Requirement 3]

💰 Salary: [Range or "Negotiable"]
📅 Deadline: [Date]

📩 How to Apply:
[Instructions - email/call/visit]

---
#JobSolutionPokhara #PokharaJobs #[Category]Jobs
```

### Image Guidelines
- Use consistent template/branding
- Include company logo if available
- Keep text readable on mobile
- Canva template for consistency

---

## SOP 3: Handling Inquiries

### From Job Seekers
- Redirect to employer contact
- Don't make promises about hiring
- Be helpful but set expectations

### From Employers
- Explain how to submit jobs
- Discuss featured/paid options (when available)
- Follow up if they seem interested

### Common Questions & Answers
| Question | Response |
|----------|----------|
| "Is this job real?" | "We verify employers before posting. Contact them directly." |
| "Can you recommend me?" | "We just share listings. Apply directly to the employer." |
| "How much to post a job?" | "Currently free. Submit via [form link]." |

---

## SOP 4: Quality Control

### Red Flags - Don't Post
- Asks for money from job seekers
- Vague company info
- Too-good-to-be-true salary
- Requests personal documents upfront
- MLM/Network marketing disguised as jobs

### When Unsure
- Google the company name
- Ask for more details
- Check if they have social media presence
- Trust your instincts
