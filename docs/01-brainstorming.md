# Brainstorming & Ideas

## The Opportunity

Pokhara is Nepal's second-largest city with:
- Growing tourism industry
- Emerging IT/tech scene
- Many small-medium businesses
- Large student population (colleges, universities)
- Limited digital job platforms focused locally

## Target Audiences

### Job Seekers
- Fresh graduates looking for first job
- Experienced professionals seeking better opportunities
- Part-time job seekers (students)
- People returning to Pokhara from Kathmandu/abroad

### Employers
- Hotels & restaurants (tourism sector)
- Retail shops & businesses
- IT companies & startups
- NGOs & INGOs
- Educational institutions
- Healthcare facilities

## Potential Features

### MVP (Start Simple)
- Job listings with basic filters (category, location, type)
- Employer submission form
- Facebook integration for reach

### Future Features
- Job seeker profiles/CV database
- Email/SMS alerts for new jobs
- Employer dashboard
- Application tracking
- Skill-based matching

## Revenue Ideas

1. **Free to start** - Build trust and volume first
2. **Featured listings** - Charge for premium placement
3. **Urgent hiring badge** - Quick visibility for time-sensitive roles
4. **Employer subscriptions** - Monthly plans for bulk posting
5. **CV database access** - Charge employers to search candidates
6. **Training/courses** - Partner with training centers

## Questions to Answer

1. What job categories are most in demand in Pokhara?
2. How do employers currently find employees? (Word of mouth? Facebook?)
3. What's the typical salary range for common positions?
4. Are job seekers willing to create profiles/upload CVs?
5. Would employers pay for job postings?
